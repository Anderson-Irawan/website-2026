My Process

#1

Discovery

An initial sit down session where we both sit in a call and discuss what the brand is, what our aims are, how we plan to accomplish, and what the deliverables are.

#2

Onboarding

After our initial discussion, I will send over a proposal to properly onboard you. The proposal will consist of a summary of our discussion. Once 40% of the deposit is paid, I will proceed with starting the project.

#3

Creative Direction

This phase consists of sketching and brainstorm, I will develop a moodboard as well as some sketches to present to you. You will receive up to 3-4 concept iterations of the final output.

#4

Development

Once a concept is agreed upon, I will start to develop the project onwards for an agreed time duration depending on the project scale and needs. This is where the real magic happens.

#5

Delivery

Once the deliverables are completed, you will be allowed to make 1 major revision and a few minor revisions before I send over the high-quality image file deliverables alongside the vector files and guidelines.
